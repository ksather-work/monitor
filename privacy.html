Comms Monitor

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Comms Monitor is a private email organization and reporting utility.

Information accessed

With authorization, the application uses Gmail read-only access to retrieve email messages and related information, such as sender, subject, date, and content. It does not send, change, or delete email.

Use of information

Information is used to organize selected messages, maintain private records, and create reports for authorized use. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Selected information may be retained in access-controlled storage and processed by third-party infrastructure services. Access is restricted to authorized operators. Reports may be shared through internal collaboration services with designated recipients. Email contents are not published on this website.

Retention and deletion

Information may be retained until deleted by the operator. Account owners can revoke access in their Google Account settings. Revoking access does not automatically remove previously stored records. Contact the address below to request deletion.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.
